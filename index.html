Hello, and welcome

Your privacy matters to us. This page tells you, in plain words, what happens to your information when you visit Amara Katha Rasa.

The short version: this website is a place to meet our chocolates. It has no accounts, no checkout, no sign-up forms, and no analytics or advertising trackers. We do not set cookies. There is no sign-up form on this site and nothing tracks you. If you want news, you write to us and we write back.

What we receive, and why

  • Your messages. When you email us or message us on Instagram, we see your name or handle, your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply, and to let you know when we open if you asked us to.
  • Basic visit details. Like most websites, our host may keep ordinary server logs (your IP address, browser type and the time of your visit). They help keep the site safe and running well.

Friends who help us

  • Google Fonts provides the lettering you see here, so your browser says hello to Google when the page loads.
  • Gmail and Instagram carry the messages you send us, and look after them under their own privacy policies.
  • Our website host delivers this page to you and keeps the logs mentioned above.

We never sell your information or share it for anything other than answering you.

How long we keep it

We keep your messages for as long as we need to look after your request. If you would like them deleted sooner, just ask.

Your choices

You are always in charge of your information. You can ask us what we hold, have it corrected or deleted, or ask us to stop writing to you. Email us at amarakatharasa@gmail.com and we will get back to you soon. You also have the rights that India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 gives you.

Young visitors

This site is made for grown-ups, so we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If a young person has written to us, let us know and we will delete the message.

When we open for orders

Taking orders means handling addresses and payments. Before that day, we will update this page so you know exactly what we collect and who helps us with it. We will ask you first before using any tracking cookies.

If something is not right

Please tell us. Write to amarakatharasa@gmail.com with the subject "Grievance". Our address: to be shared before our first order.

Made with care

We want everyone who enjoys our chocolates to feel safe and happy. Here is what we think you should know.

Allergens

Chocolate and the things we pair with it often involve milk, nuts, soy and gluten. Some chocolates may be made where nuts are also handled, so we cannot promise any product is completely free of allergen traces.

If you or the person you are gifting to has a food allergy, please write to us before ordering. We will gladly share everything we know.

What you will see on every pack

Each pack will carry its ingredients, allergen information, net weight, price, date of manufacture, best-before date and vegetarian mark, as Indian food labelling rules ask. We will share the same details here for each chocolate when it goes on sale.

Licences and approvals

We follow FSSAI terms and conditions. Our licences and approvals are still awaited, and once everything is in place, we will launch our chocolates with safety and good practice.

FSSAI licence number: will be shown here once issued.

Keeping chocolate happy

Chocolate likes a cool, dry spot, away from sunlight and strong smells. Storage tips for each product will be on its pack.
